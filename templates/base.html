EV Workplace Glossary & Concepts

Every term explained in plain English with everyday analogies

kW (Kilowatt) — Power / Speed

The rate at which electricity flows right now. Think of it like water flowing through a garden hose or speedometer in a car.

kWh (Kilowatt-hour) — Energy / Battery Tank

The total amount of electricity stored or delivered over time. Like gallons of fuel in a gas tank or battery percentage on your smartphone.

Power Cap — Site Connection Limit

The maximum total electrical power (e.g. 150 kW) the workplace grid connection can handle at any single instant. If total car charging exceeds this cap, the building circuit breaker trips.

Laxity — Spare Time Cushion

How much idle time a car has before it must charge to finish by departure. A high laxity means the car can wait; zero or negative laxity means it must charge at maximum speed immediately.

Priority Score — Smart Fairness Score

A transparent composite score made of three parts: Charge Deficit (how empty the battery is), Urgency (required speed vs car's maximum rate), and Time Left until departure.

Uncontrolled Charging — Naive Baseline

The default behavior where every car draws full power the second it plugs in, regardless of site limits. This causes severe peak spikes that breach the power cap.

Stand-in Data — Transparent Real-World Fallback

When real historical drivers did not type a requested departure time into the Caltech app, their actual unplug time and delivered kWh are used as ground-truth stand-ins.
