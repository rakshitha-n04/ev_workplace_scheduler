Honesty & Limitations Notice (E10)

• Stand-in requests: Where historical drivers did not enter their request, the actual delivered energy and unplug time are used as ground-truth stand-ins (100% of sessions in this trace).

• Departure certainty: Using actual unplug time as the deadline means the scheduler knows exactly when each car leaves. Real deployments handle uncertainty via declared departure buffers.

• Charging rate assumption: ACN-Data does not record a per-car maximum charging rate, so 6.6 kW per port is standard (configurable).

• Vehicle Variety Layers: Caltech ACN-Data contains mostly cars (Layer A). EV bikes, scooters, and vans appear in the live registered-user fleet (Layer B).
